02 / MEASUREMENT

What the numbers mean

Each project's overall percentage comes from the weighted acceptance gates in its own site-progress.json. Only fully accepted gates earn credit. Partially implemented and planned gates remain at zero until accepted. The figure does not measure elapsed time or promise a delivery date.
